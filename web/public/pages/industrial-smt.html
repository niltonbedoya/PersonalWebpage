Industrial SMT/THT

Systems of Inspection

  • Paraming: 3D SPI (paste), 3D AOI (components/soldering), 3D PSI (conformal coating)
  • Marantz: 2D inspection pre-reflow and post-reflow (SMD and THT)
  • Ersa VERSAEYE: Optical inspection of soldering
  • Matrix: In-line X-ray (AXI) for hidden solderings (BGA, QFN, voiding)

Thermal Systems & Soldering

  • Ersa: Selective soldering, wave soldering, convection ovens
  • Electrovert: Vectra wave series, reflow ovens

Metallurgy: Solders SAC305, SN100C, SnPb; Granulometry type 3/4/5

Paste reology: No-clean/soluble fluxes, type 3/4/5 particle size

Handling & Automation:

  • Loaders, unloaders, buffers, conveyor belts
  • PLC diagnosis and programming
Scope Boundary: Strict exclusion of Pick & Place machinery (no SMT placement systems included in v1)
